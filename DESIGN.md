# JevFilterForX Promo Video

## Style Prompt

Signal Cut is a dark, precise product-promo identity for filtering noisy social feeds into a clear information stream. The visual language is editorial technology: a real X feed stays documentary and legible, while the surrounding frame uses restrained cyan/teal signal marks, sharp measurement lines, and deliberate motion. The contrast should feel useful and confident, not flashy.

## Colors

- `#0b1422` — deep navy canvas and framing
- `#13243a` — elevated panels and browser chrome treatment
- `#18d6c3` — JevFilterForX signal accent
- `#0bb8f2` — secondary cyan highlight
- `#f4f8fb` — primary text and clean contrast

## Typography

- `IBM Plex Sans` — product statements and UI labels, using 500–800 weights
- `IBM Plex Mono` — scores, timings, and technical readouts, using 400–600 weights

## Motion

Precise entries with `expo.out` and `power3.out`, restrained cyan pulse accents, and medium-energy push transitions between proof points. Keep the browser feed stable and readable; animate the framing and callouts around it.

## What NOT to Do

- Do not show browser chrome, bookmarks, profile data, or unrelated page controls outside the captured main content.
- Do not obscure the X feed with oversized captions or decorative overlays.
- Do not use rainbow gradients, glitch effects, or generic neon cyberpunk decoration.
- Do not invent product claims that are not demonstrated by the extension interface.
